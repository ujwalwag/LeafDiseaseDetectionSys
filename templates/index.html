Leaf Disease Detection System

Upload a photo of a crop leaf and the app will suggest which disease or healthy class best matches it, using deep-learning models trained on a multi-class plant disease dataset. A short text description is also generated to help you interpret the result.

How to use this page

  1. Read the supported plants and conditions below so you pick a realistic test image.
  2. Choose a model from the dropdown (only models with weight files on the server are listed). The selected model loads when you run detection; only one model is kept in memory at a time to save RAM—the first prediction after a switch may take longer.
  3. Upload a JPG or PNG image showing a single leaf in reasonable focus and lighting.
  4. Click Detect Disease and review the predicted label, confidence score, and description.

Tip: results are most meaningful when the leaf clearly resembles one of the trained categories (similar to standard plant disease image benchmarks). Random objects or unrelated plants may receive low-confidence or misleading labels.
